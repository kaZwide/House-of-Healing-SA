Meet Your Practitioner

MEET YOUR PRACTITIONER

Gogo Nyoniyamanzi

At the heart of House of Healing SA is Gogo Nyoniyamanzi (Yvonne Lerato Mogotsi), a traditional healing practitioner with over 15 years of experience and a deep passion for holistic wellness.

Her journey is rooted in cultural heritage, traditional knowledge, and a commitment to compassionate, individual care.

Her professional background includes training in traditional medicine, cupping, reflexology, and first aid, alongside further studies in medicine at the University of the Witwatersrand.

Gogo Nyoniyamanzi is a registered traditional healer (Registration No. 013-489, as supplied by the practitioner). Her approach is guided by experience, respect, and an appreciation for each individual's wellness journey.

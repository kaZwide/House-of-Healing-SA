Marine Collagen

Marine Collagen is a dietary supplement made from hydrolysed collagen peptides sourced from fish. Collagen is an important structural protein naturally found in the skin, bones, joints and connective tissues.

Marine collagen is commonly used in beauty and wellness routines to support skin hydration and elasticity.
